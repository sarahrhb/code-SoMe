About the board

What is it?

A shared place for SoMe Incubator members to ask for help with editing, filming, design, copywriting, or feedback—and to find requests they can help with.

How it works

  1. Browse the requests or ask the community for help.
  2. Apply to help with a request. The requester can accept or decline.
  3. Check Updates for applications and decisions. Accepted projects appear in My help.
  4. Mark your request Done when it is finished. Put its receipt in the bin to hide it from the board while keeping its updates.

Please note

You need an account to post or apply. Requests are visible to everyone. If you apply, the requester sees your CODE email only after you agree to share it. Keep private information out of requests. Replies and decisions appear under Updates; the board does not send email notifications.
