About this board

What is it?

A shared space for creators in the CODE incubator. You can ask other members for help with editing, filming, design, copywriting or feedback.

How it works

  1. Look at the open requests.
  2. Post your own request and say what you need.
  3. Apply to help with someone else's request. The requester can accept or decline.
  4. Check Updates for applications, decisions, withdrawals and requests marked done.
  5. Change your request to "Done" when it is finished.

Please note

You need an account to post. You can edit or delete only requests made by your account. If you apply to help, the requester will see your CODE email so they can contact you. Do not post private information.
