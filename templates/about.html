About the board

What is it?

A shared place for SoMe Incubator members to ask for help with editing, filming, design, copywriting, or feedback.

How it works

  1. Browse the requests or post a request.
  2. Apply to help with a request. The requester can accept or decline.
  3. Check Updates for applications and decisions. Accepted projects appear in "My help".
  4. Mark your request "Done" when it is finished. Put its receipt in the bin to hide it from the board.

Please note

You need an account to post or apply. Requests are visible to everyone. If you apply, the requester sees your CODE email to contact you for help. !!Keep private information out of requests!! Replies and decisions appear under Updates.
